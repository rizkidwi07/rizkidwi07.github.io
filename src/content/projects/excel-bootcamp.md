## Context

The final portfolio of the MySkill Intensive Bootcamp (Fullstack Microsoft Excel, Basic to Advanced, Batch 18). The final score of 91 combines attendance (93), mini tasks (99), and the post test (82).

## Part 1: formulas and cleaning

Formulas, text and date functions, data cleansing and formatting, and IF, IFS, and nested IF logic. In one case study I rated salespeople as Excellent, Good, or Bad from their sales count and total sales using nested IF with AND and OR.

## Part 2: validation and lookups

Data validation lists with the circle-invalid-data check, conditional formatting rules, charts (bar, line, pie, and combo), and VLOOKUP and INDEX-MATCH to join student records across sheets.

## Part 3: pivot tables and a dashboard

On a retail sales dataset I built pivot tables for:

- sales per year,
- sales and profit by ship mode,
- the top 10 products by quantity,
- a calculated field for profit as a percentage of sales, with a filter for customers at 40% or more,
- sales by city and by product category.

The final task combines them into a dashboard with a year slicer, a customer lookup, monthly sales history, sales contribution by category, and sales by region and segment.

## Limitations

These are guided course exercises on a sample dataset, not work on real business data. I include them to show my spreadsheet analysis foundation next to the Python projects.
